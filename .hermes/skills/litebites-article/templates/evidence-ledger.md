## Review rules

- A search result, snippet, or generated summary is not evidence; open the canonical source.
- Preserve the responsible party's scope and tense. A roadmap is not a release; a demo is not a benchmark.
- Record exact dates and versions when the story depends on timing.
- Seek independent corroboration for performance, safety, adoption, compatibility, cost, or market-impact claims; if none exists, attribute the claim and state the gap.
- Resolve contradictions before drafting, or report them explicitly.
- Reject any claim whose source location cannot be recovered at final review.

Done when every consequential sentence planned for the Article maps to a row with adequate evidence or explicitly caveated wording.
