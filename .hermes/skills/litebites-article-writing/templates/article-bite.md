## What happened

State the verified development, responsible organization or author, exact date, and concrete scope. Attribute source claims directly.

## Why it matters

Explain the immediate technical or operational consequence. Separate present evidence from possible longer-term implications.

## Technical context

Supply only the architecture, protocol, benchmark, deployment, or standards context needed to understand the development.

## What remains uncertain

Identify missing evidence, unavailable benchmarks, compatibility limits, incentives, or claims that have not been independently verified.

## Practical takeaways

- Provide three to five concrete observations or checks.
- Keep interpretation distinguishable from reported fact.
- Avoid generic praise and unsupported predictions.
