## Review rules

- Open and read the canonical source; a search result, snippet, or generated summary is not evidence.
- Preserve the responsible party's scope and tense. A roadmap is not a release; a demonstration is not a benchmark.
- Record exact dates and versions when the story depends on timing.
- Seek independent corroboration for performance, safety, adoption, compatibility, cost, or market-impact claims.
- If corroboration is unavailable, attribute the claim and state the gap.
- Resolve contradictions before drafting, or report the contradiction explicitly.
- Keep LiteBites interpretation separate from what a source says.
- Reject any claim whose source location cannot be recovered during final review.

Completion criterion: every consequential sentence planned for the Article Bite maps to a row, and every row has either adequate evidence or explicit caveated wording.
